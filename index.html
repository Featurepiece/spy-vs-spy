How to play

  • You are Scarlet (top). The computer is Cobalt (bottom).
  • Search furniture for the passport, money, key and plans. Without the briefcase you can carry only one thing.
  • Pick a trap, then set it on furniture (bomb, spring) or in a doorway (bucket, gun). The time bomb goes off 15 s after you drop it. Your own traps catch you too.
  • Remedies hang in the embassy: the fire box (water), tool box (cutters), coat rack (umbrella) and first aid kit (scissors). Carry the right one and a trap is defused.
  • Meet your rival and it's a club fight: no searching or traps. A knockout hides the loser's items in that room.
  • Arrows under your room point the way back through the last 9 rooms (Levels 1 and 2).
  • Getting caught costs 27 s of your clock. Run out of time and the plane leaves without you.
